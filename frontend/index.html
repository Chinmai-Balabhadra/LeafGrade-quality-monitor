System Capabilities

Engineered for Agricultural Precision

Designed to address the unique challenges of outdoor handheld leaf inspection under varying daylight, shadows, and angle variations.

MobileNetV2 Architecture

Lightweight, inverted-residual convolutional neural network with depthwise separable convolutions optimized for edge inference on handheld devices.

Live Camera & Image Ingestion

Seamlessly integrates desktop file selection, mobile drag-and-drop, and in-browser camera streaming via HTML5 MediaDevices APIs.

Blur & Sharpness Guardrails

Calculates Laplacian variance on incoming frames to detect camera defocus and motion blur, warning farmers before inaccurate decisions are made.

Maturity Standard Separation

Strictly preserves the agronomic distinction between visual health and harvesting maturity. Healthy foliage is never automatically deemed ready to pluck.

Processing Pipeline

How the System Evaluates Leaves

A structured four-stage computer vision workflow transforming raw imagery into verified agronomic guidance.

1

Capture / Ingestion

The user uploads or photographs a single leaf under outdoor ambient lighting using a smartphone or digital camera.

2

Quality Preprocessing

The image is inspected for motion blur, converted from BGR to natural RGB, and adaptively resized to the 224×224 neural input standard.

3

Deep Neural Inference

The fine-tuned MobileNetV2 network extracts convolutional feature maps and generates a calibrated 28-class Softmax probability distribution.

4

Agronomic Advisory

Confidence thresholding gates uncertainty, ranks the Top-3 candidates, and pairs visual health with plucking maturity recommendations.

Academic Project

About the CSE471 Project

Developed as part of the academic curriculum in Computer Science and Engineering.

In commercial plantations and precision agriculture (such as tea, herbs, and leafy vegetables), the timing of leaf harvest directly dictates crop grade, chemical composition, and market value. Conventional inspection relies exclusively on subjective manual labor, leading to high variance in plucking standards and missed early disease outbreaks.

This system implements an end-to-end computer vision solution trained on the benchmark PlantDoc dataset (2,916 cleaned and deduplicated images across 28 plant pathology classes). By combining transfer learning with agronomic safeguards, the system achieves reliable diagnostic accuracy while strictly preventing false harvesting approvals.

Core Tech Stack: Python 3.12, TensorFlow 2.21, Keras 3.15, MobileNetV2, OpenCV 5.0, Flask 3.1, Vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript.
